Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does TrekPal require an account or internet connection?

A: No! TrekPal works 100% offline. No accounts, logins, passwords, or personal setups are ever required. All time zones, phrasebook translations, tipping customs, and safety guides are stored entirely on your device.

Q: How do I add, remove, or reorder cities?

A: Tap the search icon (+) on the top right to search across 10,000+ global cities. Long-press or use the reorder icon to drag cities into your preferred order. Swipe left or tap the red minus icon to delete a city.

Q: How do live currency rates update?

A: When an internet connection is available, TrekPal retrieves public foreign exchange rate tables from open.er-api.com anonymously. These rates are cached offline so you can calculate conversions on planes, trains, or off-grid travels.

Q: How do I replay the interactive guided tours?

A: Go to Settings and tap DEMO RESET. This allows you to replay interactive animations and walkthroughs anytime.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TrekPal handles user privacy and personal data. We believe in absolute privacy: our application is engineered to collect zero personal information.

1. Zero Data Collection & Local Storage

TrekPal does not harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). All custom cities, color themes, language selections, and travel notes stay strictly within your device's sandboxed local storage (UserDefaults).

2. Global Privacy Frameworks Compliance

TrekPal complies with all international and federal data protection legislation:

  • Canada (PIPEDA & BC PIPA): British Columbia Personal Information Protection Act and Federal PIPEDA.
  • United States (CCPA / CPRA): California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act.
  • European Union & UK (GDPR): General Data Protection Regulation (Articles 12, 13, 14, and 17).
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): Zero personal data collection from children or adults.

3. Third-Party Data Processors

TrekPal maintains a strict zero-data-selling guarantee. Our minimal third-party dependencies are:

  • Exchange Rates API (open.er-api.com): Anonymous outgoing HTTPS requests for currency rates. No user identifiers or cookies are transmitted.
  • Apple App Store: Native app delivery, crash diagnostic logs (if voluntarily enabled via iOS Settings), and on-device text-to-speech audio synthesis.

4. AI & Automated Processing Disclosures

TrekPal's calculations, phrasebook translations, and tipping etiquette are deterministic and processed entirely on-device. No private user searches, notes, or interactions are ever used to train public or foundation AI models.

5. 100% Free — No Subscriptions

TrekPal is 100% free with no auto-renewing subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no payment card information is ever collected or processed.

6. Right to Erasure

You can reset all local storage at any time via the DEMO RESET feature or by deleting the app from your device, which instantly purges all local data.
