Frequently Asked Questions

Does TrekPal require an internet connection?

No. TrekPal is designed to work 100% offline. Time zones, phrasebooks, tipping customs, and safety guides are stored entirely on your device.

How do I add or reorder cities?

Tap the search (+) icon in the top navigation bar to add any city worldwide. In Edit mode, use the reorder handles to arrange cities in your preferred order.

How are currency rates updated?

When connected to the internet, TrekPal anonymously fetches public foreign exchange rate tables from open.er-api.com and caches them locally for offline travel.

How do I reset guided tours?

Go to the Settings tab and tap DEMO RESET to replay the interactive tours anytime.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

TrekPal is designed with user privacy as a fundamental priority. Our application operates strictly as a local-first utility and collects zero personal information.

Zero Data Collection Guarantee: TrekPal does not harvest, store, track, sell, or share any personal user information, location data, or device identifiers.

1. Local Storage

All user preferences—including added cities, selected color palettes, language settings, and custom notes—are stored exclusively in sandboxed local storage on your device (iOS UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

2. Legal & Regulatory Compliance

TrekPal complies with global and regional data protection regulations:

  • Canada: Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and BC Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).
  • United States: California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).
  • European Union & UK: General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR).
  • Children's Privacy: Compliant with COPPA standards (no data collected).

3. Third-Party Services

TrekPal only communicates with:

  • Currency Exchange API (open.er-api.com): Anonymous outgoing HTTPS requests to retrieve public foreign exchange rates. No personal identifiers or cookies are sent.
  • Apple App Store: Standard application distribution and optional crash analytics (opt-in only via iOS Settings).

4. AI & Automated Systems

TrekPal does not use user inputs, notes, or searches to train any public or foundation artificial intelligence models.

5. Free Application & Billing

TrekPal is 100% free. There are no paid tiers, no auto-renewing subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no credit card information is ever collected.

6. Data Deletion

You can reset all local storage at any time using the DEMO RESET feature in Settings, or by uninstalling the application from your device.
