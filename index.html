Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does TrekPal require an internet connection?

No. TrekPal is designed as a 100% offline-first utility. Time zones, phrasebooks, tipping etiquette, and safety guidelines are stored entirely on your device.

2. How are currency rates updated?

When connected to the internet, TrekPal anonymously fetches public foreign exchange rate tables from open.er-api.com and caches them locally for offline travel.

3. How do I change target time offsets?

Swipe horizontally on your dashboard to shift target time across all countries. Tap the circular reset arrow in the header to return to current live time.

4. How does the Travel Phrasebook pronunciation work?

Tap the speech bubble icon on any country card to hear natural, calm female voice pronunciation across international phrases.

5. How do I delete all my data and reset the app?

Open the Settings tab in the app and tap "ERASE ALL DATA & RESET". Confirm the prompt to immediately wipe all local preferences, saved cities, and custom notes.

Legal & Compliance
