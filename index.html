Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Does TrekPal require an active internet connection?

No. TrekPal is designed as a 100% offline-first utility. Time zone offsets, travel phrasebooks, tipping guidelines, and tap water safety advisories are packaged directly on your device.

2. How are currency exchange rates updated?

When an internet connection is present at launch, TrekPal anonymously fetches public foreign exchange rate tables from open.er-api.com and caches them locally for offline travel. If offline, the app automatically uses internal fallback rates.

3. How do I adjust target time offsets?

Swipe horizontally (left or right) anywhere on your dashboard list to preview future or past hours across all saved countries. Tap the counter-clockwise circular reset button in the header to return to live time.

4. How does the Travel Phrasebook audio pronunciation work?

Tap any speech bubble on your country cards to hear natural, warm female voice pronunciations (powered by advanced Kokoro TTS and enhanced female voice synthesis pipelines) with natural human pacing across international dialects.

5. How do I delete all my data and restore factory defaults?

Open the Settings tab in the app and tap "ERASE ALL DATA & RESET". Confirm the prompt to immediately wipe all local preferences, saved cities, and custom notes.
