Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I change the time offset?

Swipe horizontally (left or right) anywhere on your dashboard list. To return to live time, tap the circular reset arrow in the header.

2. How do I reorder country cards?

Press and hold any country card for 0.3 seconds until it elevates, then drag up or down to your desired position.

3. Does currency conversion work offline?

Yes. TrekPal caches the latest exchange rates and includes a built-in offline currency table for continuous travel use.

4. How does the Travel Phrasebook pronunciation work?

Tap the speech bubble icon on any country card to hear natural, calm female voice pronunciation across international phrases.

5. How do I change the app language?

Open the Settings tab in the bottom toolbar and select your preferred language. This automatically translates all phrases and tipping guidelines.
