Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I change the time offset to preview a future date/time?

Simply swipe horizontally (left or right) anywhere on your dashboard list. The timezone card headers will show the updated preview time. To reset back to the current live system time, tap the counter-clockwise circular arrow icon in the top header.

2. How do I reorder my country cards?

Press and hold down on any country card for 0.3 seconds. Once the card scales slightly and elevates, drag it up or down to your desired position in the sequence, then release.

3. Why aren't my currency rates updating?

TrekPal requires an active internet connection on launch to fetch the latest global exchange rates from our secure API partner. If you are offline, the app automatically falls back to secure, built-in offline currency exchange rates so that you can continue converting currencies without interruption.

4. How does the Travel Phrasebook audio pronunciation work?

Tap the speech bubble icon on any country card to hear high-quality, natural human-like female voice pronunciation (powered by advanced Kokoro TTS and enhanced female voice synthesis pipelines). The app speaks travel phrases with natural pacing and warm cadence.

5. How do I change my native language?

Tap the Settings tab in the bottom toolbar, then select your preferred language. This automatically translates all travel phrases and local tipping customs across 18 languages.
